| **Chemotherapy agents (if ongoing)** | Some flavonoids (e.g., high‑dose curcumin) can alter cytochrome‑P450; discuss with oncologist. |
| **Renal or hepatic impairment** | Adjust doses of magnesium, ALCAR, high‑dose B‑vitamins; obtain labs before starting. |

## 6. Lifestyle adjuncts that synergise with supplementation

1. **Cognitive “rehab”** – Structured memory exercises (spaced repetition, dual‑n‑back, mnemonic strategies) 3‑5 × /week improve neuro‑plasticity.  
2. **Aerobic activity** – 30 min moderate‑intensity walking or cycling ≥3 × /week enhances hippocampal blood flow and BDNF.  
3. **Sleep optimisation** – Aim for 7–9 h, maintain regular bedtime, limit caffeine after 14:00. Sleep is essential for memory consolidation.  
4. **Stress reduction** – Mindfulness or progressive muscle relaxation reduces cortisol, which can otherwise impair hippocampal function.
